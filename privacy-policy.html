Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how Ghungroo Kathak Academy collects and uses your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Fill out our contact or enrollment forms.
  • Email us directly for inquiries.
  • Register for classes or examinations.
This may include your name, email address, phone number, and any other details you choose to share.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide information about our courses.
  • To manage student enrollments and registrations.
  • To communicate important updates regarding classes, schedules, and exams.
  • To improve our website experience through analytics.

3. Cookies and Third-Party Tracking

Our website uses cookies to improve your browsing experience. We may use third-party services, such as:

  • Google Analytics: To understand how visitors interact with our site.
  • Google Ads: To reach potential students who are interested in Kathak and dance.
  • Instagram Feed: To display our latest academy moments.
These services may use cookies to track visits and serve relevant advertisements. You can control cookie settings in your browser.

4. Data Protection & Security

We are committed to ensuring that your information is secure. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is used only for the operational needs of the Ghungroo Kathak Academy.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, or to request that we correct or delete your information. Please contact us to make such a request.
